NOTE ON SIZE/SHAPE:
All photo boxes in the redesigned CSS are set to a LANDSCAPE
aspect ratio (16:9, and the hero photo is a portrait 3:4). For the
sharpest result, crop or export your source photos in the matching
orientation before placing them here — images will still be cropped
automatically to fit (object-fit: cover), but starting from the
right orientation avoids awkward cropping of faces/subjects.

NOTE ON THE SCROLLING CARDS:
Two cards now show a continuously auto-scrolling filmstrip instead of
a single static image:
  - "Believers (Quadrice)" in NPTEL STAR — 4 photos (photo5-1.jpg to
    photo5-4.jpg), portrait/certificate framing.
  - "Best Faculty Award (2022-23, 2023-24, 2025-26)" — 3 photos
    (photo14-1.jpg to photo14-3.jpg), landscape framing.
Both loop seamlessly and pause automatically while the visitor hovers
over the card so they can look at whichever photo is showing.

Until you add real files here, the page will automatically fall back
to placeholder stock images (this is already built into the HTML via
the onerror attribute on each <img> tag), so the site still looks
complete even with this folder empty.
